Support

Email connorpace14@gmail.com and you'll get a reply. It's one person, not a support desk, so please allow a few days — and within 30 days for anything about your data.

It helps to include your phone and OS version, and the app's version number — you'll find it at the very bottom of the Terms · Privacy · Your data screen, reached from the bottom of the Home screen.

Common questions

Do I have to sign in?

No. Skip sign in keeps everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. You can sign in later and what you've already written comes with you. The trade-off is that a local-only journal disappears if you lose or wipe the phone.

How do I delete my account and everything in it?

In the app: Home screen → Terms · Privacy · Your data at the bottom → Delete my account. It asks twice, and spells out exactly what goes. It removes every entry, photo, voice note, prayer, milestone, habit record and your story, from the phone and from the account, and removes the sign-in record itself. It cannot be undone, so export anything you want to keep as a PDF first.

If some of it can't be reached — usually because the phone is offline — the app tells you what's left rather than claiming a clean wipe. Try again with a connection, or email the address above and it will be removed for you.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Everything you write saves to the phone the moment you type it. When there's no connection the app says so at the top of the screen, and the two things that do need one — looking up a verse, and backing up to your account — catch up on their own once you're back online.

Why isn't the NIV / ESV / NLT available?

Those translations are licensed commercially by their publishers. The app offers only translations that are free to quote — WEB, WEBBE, BBE, ASV, YLT, Darby and the KJV.

Can I get my writing out of the app?

Any entry exports as a notebook-styled PDF, with the photos in the places you put them. Prayers, milestones and your story share as text through your phone's own share sheet.

I lost my entries after reinstalling

If you were signed in, sign in again with the same account and everything comes back. If you were using the app without signing in, the writing lived only on that phone and there is no copy to restore — which is why the app offers sign-in.

Something's broken, or I have an idea

Please email it. Bugs get fixed faster with a description of what you tapped and what happened instead, and feature requests are genuinely read.
